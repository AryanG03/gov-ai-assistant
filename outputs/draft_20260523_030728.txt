SUBJECT: Compliance with Circular issued by Government of Maharashtra — Action Taken Report

Sir/Madam,

Subject: Compliance with Circular No. FIN/2024/001 - Monthly Utilization Certificates and Separate Accounts for Scheme Funds

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing in reference to the recently issued circular, numbered FIN/2024/001, which outlines the obligations of all government offices receiving state funds. Pursuant to this directive, I would like to confirm our office's compliance with the stipulated guidelines.

In accordance with the requirements set forth in the circular, we hereby commit to submitting monthly utilization certificates by the 5th of each month. These certificates will provide a detailed account of how the allocated funds have been utilized during the preceding month.

Furthermore, separate accounts for all scheme funds have been established as per the guidelines. We understand that maintaining distinct accounts is crucial to ensure transparency and efficient management of state funds.

In case of any irregularities or discrepancies, we shall immediately report them to the Finance Secretary for appropriate action. It is our responsibility to uphold the integrity of the financial system and adhere to the guidelines set forth by the authorities.

As per the circular, this directive is applicable to all government offices receiving state funds within the jurisdiction of the Government of Maharashtra, under the Finance Department. The first submission deadline for monthly utilization certificates is set for 05-February-2024.

We assure you that our office will diligently follow these guidelines and adhere to all deadlines in order to maintain transparency and accountability in the management of state funds.

Yours faithfully,
