SUBJECT: Compliance with Circular issued by Government of Maharashtra — Action Taken Report

Sir/Madam,

Subject: Compliance with Circular No. FIN/2024/001 - Monthly Utilization Certificates and Separate Accounts for Scheme Funds

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing to confirm our office's compliance with the recently issued circular, numbered FIN/2024/001, by the Finance Department of the Government of Maharashtra. Pursuant to this directive, we hereby commit to adhering to all stipulations outlined within.

In accordance with the guidelines provided in the circular, we shall submit monthly utilization certificates for all state-funded projects by the 5th of each month. These certificates will provide a detailed account of how the allocated funds have been utilized during the preceding month. This will ensure transparency and facilitate efficient monitoring of project progress.

Furthermore, we confirm that separate accounts have been established for all scheme funds as per your instructions. This is in line with the circular's mandate to maintain a clear distinction between different funds to prevent any misappropriation or mixing of resources.

In case of any irregularities or suspected fraudulent activities, we shall immediately report them to the Finance Secretary for prompt action and resolution. We understand that it is our responsibility to uphold the integrity of the financial systems in place and take all necessary measures to prevent any misuse of state funds.

We would like to reiterate that this circular applies to all government offices receiving state funds within our jurisdiction, ensuring uniformity and consistency across all departments. The first submission deadline for monthly utilization certificates is set for the 5th of February 2024.

Please accept our assurances that we will continue to adhere to these guidelines in letter and spirit, upholding the highest standards of financial accountability and transparency.

Yours faithfully,
