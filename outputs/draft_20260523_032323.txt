SUBJECT: Compliance with Circular issued by Government of Maharashtra — Action Taken Report

Sir/Madam,

Subject: Compliance with Circular No. FIN/2024/001 - Monthly Utilization Certificates and Separate Accounts for Scheme Funds

Sir/Madam,

I am writing to confirm our office's compliance with the recently issued circular, numbered FIN/2024/001, by the Finance Department of the Government of Maharashtra. Pursuant to this directive, we hereby commit to adhering to the stipulated guidelines.

In accordance with the requirements outlined in the circular, we shall submit monthly utilization certificates by the 5th of each month. These certificates will provide a detailed account of how the state funds have been utilized during the preceding month. This will ensure transparency and facilitate efficient monitoring of fund usage.

Furthermore, separate accounts for all scheme funds have been established in accordance with the circular's directives. We understand that maintaining distinct accounts is crucial to prevent any commingling of funds and to enable a clear audit trail.

In case of any irregularities or discrepancies, we shall report them immediately to the Finance Secretary to ensure prompt resolution and adherence to the highest standards of financial integrity.

We would like to reiterate that this circular is applicable to all government offices receiving state funds within our district. As such, we request all concerned departments to familiarize themselves with the guidelines and abide by them.

The first submission in accordance with the circular's requirements is due by 05-February-2024. We shall ensure that all necessary documentation and reports are prepared and submitted well ahead of this deadline.

Thank you for your attention to this matter, and we look forward to a continued partnership in upholding the highest standards of financial management and transparency.

Yours faithfully,
[Designation]
