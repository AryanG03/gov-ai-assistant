SUBJECT: Compliance with Circular issued by Government of Maharashtra — Action Taken Report

Sir/Madam,

Subject: Compliance with Circular No. FIN/2024/001 - Monthly Utilization Certificates and Separate Accounts for Scheme Funds

Dear Sir/Madam,

I am writing in reference to the recently issued circular, numbered FIN/2024/001, by the Finance Department of the Government of Maharashtra. This circular outlines specific obligations and deadlines that all government offices receiving state funds must adhere to.

Pursuant to this directive, I hereby confirm that our office shall submit monthly utilization certificates by the 5th of each month, starting with the first submission due on the 5th of February 2024. These certificates will provide a detailed account of how the allocated funds have been utilized during the previous month.

In accordance with the requirements outlined in the circular, separate accounts for all scheme funds have already been established to ensure proper management and transparency of these funds. These accounts are maintained in compliance with the prescribed banking procedures and auditing standards.

Should any irregularities or discrepancies be discovered during the course of our duties, we shall immediately report such findings to the Finance Secretary for appropriate action.

As per the circular, this directive is applicable to all government offices receiving state funds within the jurisdiction of the District Collectors in Maharashtra. We are committed to adhering to these guidelines and maintaining the highest standards of financial accountability and transparency.

Thank you for your attention to this matter.

Yours faithfully,
